## 7.1 Permutations

### Recall — Permutations and Sym(X)

- A **permutation** of a set $X$ is a bijection $X\to X$.
- $\operatorname{Sym}(X)$ is the group of all such permutations ([[Groups - Chapter 1 Examples and Definitions#Definition 1.14 — The groups $S_n$|Definition 1.14]] for $X=\{1,\dots,n\}$).
